Grammar Mastery: Recounting Experiences with 5 Senses

Saat menyusun *personal recount text* tentang peristiwa olahraga, kita memadukan Simple Past Tense (alur kronologis aksi), Past Continuous Tense (latar belakang aksi yang sedang terjadi), serta Sensory Verbs (pancaindra) untuk membangkitkan suasana nyata kepada pembaca.

Fokus 1

Simple Past

Verb 2 (Regular/Irregular) & Was/Were

Fokus 2

Past Continuous

Was/Were + V-ing (When / While)

Fokus 3

Sensory Verbs

Linking Verb + Adjective (5 Indra)

Fokus 4

Time Connectors

First, then, meanwhile, suddenly

1

Simple Past Tense

Peristiwa olahraga yang telah tuntas terjadi

Pola Verbal:

(+) Subject + Verb 2 (Regular / Irregular) + Object

(-) Subject + did not (didn't) + Verb 1

(?) Did + Subject + Verb 1?

Pola Nominal (Tanpa Kata Kerja):

I / He / She / It / Singular → was

You / We / They / Plural → were

Contoh Kalimat Olahraga:
  • We cheered as the national team scored. (Regular)
  • The striker caught the loose ball and shot. (Irregular)
  • He didn't miss a single free-throw. (Bukan: didn't missed)
Kunci Penting: Setelah kata bantu did atau didn't, kata kerja wajib kembali ke bentuk pertama (*Verb 1 / bare infinitive*).
2

Past Continuous Tense

Aksi yang sedang berlangsung di tengah pertandingan

Rumus Dasar:

(+) Subject + was/were + Verb-ing

(-) Subject + was/were not (wasn't/weren't) + Verb-ing

(?) Was/Were + Subject + Verb-ing?

Aturan Kombinasi 'When' & 'While':

• While + Past Continuous: Menunjukkan aktivitas berdurasi panjang.
• When + Simple Past: Menunjukkan kejadian seketika yang menyela aktivitas panjang tersebut.

Contoh Kalimat Olahraga:
  • Thousands of fans were waving flags when the anthem played.
  • While I was buying hotdogs, the referee blew the whistle.
Catatan: Jangan gunakan -ing pada kata kerja persepsi statif seperti hear atau see (gunakan I heard, bukan I was hearing).
3

Sensory Verbs & Adjectives

Mendeskripsikan apa yang kita rasakan di arena

Aturan Tata Bahasa Krusial:

Dalam menceritakan sensasi, kata kerja indra (taste, feel, sound, smell, look) berfungsi sebagai linking verb, sehingga diikuti oleh Adjective (Kata Sifat), bukan Adverb (akhiran -ly).

The arena sounded deafening.
The arena sounded deafeningly.
The hotdog tasted savory and delicious.
Kosakata Sensorik 5 Indra:
👀 Sight: crowded, colorful, vibrant 👂 Hearing: deafening, thunderous, loud ✋ Touch: tense, sweaty, scorching hot 👅 Taste/Smell: savory, smoky, greasy
Contoh Recount: "The weather felt scorching, but the supporters looked enthusiastic."
4

Sequence & Time Connectors

Menyusun kronologi alur babak demi babak

Keterangan Waktu (Adverbs of Time):

yesterday, last Saturday, two months ago, during the second half, in the final round.

Penghubung Urutan (Sequence Markers):

First (awal), Then / Next (lanjutan), Suddenly (kejadian mendadak), Meanwhile (bersamaan), In the end / Finally (akhir).

Penerapan Nyata:
"First, we queued at Gate 3. Then, the players entered the pitch. Meanwhile, the opposing coach prepared tactics. Suddenly, a goal was scored!"
Fungsi: Menjadikan paragraf recount koheren dan mudah ditelusuri urutan peristiwanya.

Deep Dive: Sensory Linking Verbs vs Action Verbs

Kapan kata kerja indra memakai Adjective dan kapan memakai Adverb?

1. Sebagai Linking Verb (+ Adjective)

Ketika kata kerja tersebut menggambarkan karakteristik atau keadaan yang dirasakan oleh subjek (bukan tindakan fisik aktif).

• The stadium felt hot.
• The music sounded energetic.
• The drink tasted sweet.
2. Sebagai Action Verb (+ Adverb)

Ketika subjek secara fisik melakukan tindakan sengaja menggunakan indranya terhadap suatu objek.

• The referee looked carefully at the replay.
• The dog smelled the ball eagerly.
• The athlete felt the ground gently.
